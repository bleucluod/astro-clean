# Halleus Product Surface Cleanup

This milestone makes the public product surface more coherent.

## What changed

- Added a central product surface link map.
- Added `/product` as a product map page.
- Reworked privacy, roadmap, and wiki pages around the current product stage.
- Added a product surface checker.
- Kept this phase provider-free and payment-free.

## Why this matters

The product now has a clearer public structure:

- Product map
- Chart creation
- Reports archive
- Dashboard
- Profile
- Pricing
- Privacy
- Roadmap
- Wiki

This makes the app easier to demo and easier to keep coherent as auth/database/payment are added.
