# P01 approved R9b master assets

The decorative near-black sky (p01-r9b-approved-sky.webp) was derived from the
owner-approved R9b sample image. Its sample-specific Moon and text were removed,
so new customer information is never recovered from a flattened image.
Stars and clouds in the sky are decorative, not a reconstruction of the sky
for the chosen date.

The actual lunar surface comes from an independently validated private source
loaded per order by the existing P01 print handoff; preferred map:
Solar System Scope 8192x4096 Moon map, Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 4.0,
SHA-256 d1875bcec83588ca25e4802e576f6bb9f88b39e1e403cb41ff55867419c54796.
